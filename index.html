Network Design · Tableau · Optimization

The Freshness Dilemma: Building a Global Cold-Chain Network

Designed an end-to-end cold-chain supply network to move 11.5M+ units of highly perishable Thai coconut water 8,000 miles to U.S. retailers (Costco, Trader Joe's, Whole Foods). Clustered 1,200 stores into 7 distribution centers across four port regions in Tableau, built a KPI-based supplier scorecard to select five reliable Thai farms, and ran demand scenarios (2 to 4%) with an optimization model to find the lowest-cost flow through the Los Angeles, Houston, Savannah, and New York City ports.
